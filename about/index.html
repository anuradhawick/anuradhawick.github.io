Hi! I am Anuradha 😃

Engineer, Scientist, and Researcher. Software Engineer at AML3D developing algorithms for advanced manufacturing applications (WAAM), former Solutions Architect at CSIRO, and PhD graduate from the Australian National University.

I am a Software Engineer, Scientist, and Researcher. Currently at AML3D, I engineer and develop algorithms for advanced manufacturing applications, specializing in Wire Arc Additive Manufacturing (WAAM).

Previously, I worked as a Solutions Architect at CSIRO (Australia's National Science Agency) with the Transformational Bioinformatics Group, architecting population-scale bioinformatics platforms and healthcare systems. I received my PhD in Computer Science from the Australian National University (ANU), focusing on metagenomics binning models, machine learning, and high-performance computational biology algorithms.

My expertise bridges algorithmic development for robotic metal additive manufacturing (WAAM), machine learning modelling, GPU acceleration (CUDA), and high-performance systems. But above all, I love solving difficult computational problems and writing clean code 😃.
