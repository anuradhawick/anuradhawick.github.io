Solutions Architect · Researcher · Bioinformatician

G'day! I'm Anuradha.

I work as a solutions architect building bioinformatics products. I specialize in cloud-first computational biology, high-performance GPU algorithms, and machine learning models for genomics.

GitHub LinkedIn Google Scholar Bluesky About Me →

I did my PhD at the Australian National University (ANU). My thesis was titled "Models and algorithms for metagenomics binning and plasmid classification". I love reading algorithms, tinkering on single-board computers (SBCs), travelling, and gaming (PC/XBOX/PS). On holidays, I am either driving or cooking!

Previously, I worked at CSIRO (Australia's National Science Agency) with the Transformational Bioinformatics Group. In any case, happy to connect via LinkedIn as a fellow researcher, DIY'er, programming geek, or fellow human being!
